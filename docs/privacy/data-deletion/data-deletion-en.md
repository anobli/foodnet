# Data Deletion - FoodNet

**Last updated: March 25, 2026**

## Overview

FoodNet respects your right to delete your data at any time. This page explains how to delete your data from the FoodNet application.

## Types of Data

FoodNet stores two types of data:

### 1. Local Data
Data stored on your smartphone, including:
- Food items and expiration dates
- Categories and preferences
- App settings

### 2. Cloud Data (if you're connected)
Data synchronized to our Firestore database, including:
- Your email address
- Food inventory data
- Group memberships

## How to Delete Your Data

### Deleting Cloud Data (Recommended Method)

If you are using the cloud synchronization feature, you can delete your cloud data directly from the app:

1. **Open the FoodNet app**
2. **Go to the menu** (usually accessible via the three dots or hamburger icon)
3. **Select "Delete Account"** or **"Remove Account"**
4. **Confirm your choice**

Your data will be permanently removed from our Firestore database. This includes:
- Your email address
- All synchronized food inventory data
- Group memberships and shared data

**Important:** This action is irreversible. Once deleted, your cloud data cannot be recovered.

**Note:** Simply disconnecting from the service will not delete your data from our servers. You must use the "Delete Account" or "Remove Account" option to permanently remove your data.

### Deleting Local Data

To delete data stored locally on your smartphone:

**Option 1: Clear app data**
1. Go to your device **Settings**
2. Select **Apps** or **Applications**
3. Find and select **FoodNet**
4. Tap **Storage**
5. Tap **Clear Data** or **Clear Storage**

**Option 2: Uninstall the app**
1. Go to your device **Settings**
2. Select **Apps** or **Applications**
3. Find and select **FoodNet**
4. Tap **Uninstall**

## Data Deletion Timeline

- **Cloud data**: Deleted immediately upon request from the app
- **Local data**: Deleted immediately when you clear app data or uninstall

## Backup Copies

Please note:
- We do not maintain backup copies of your data beyond what is stored in Firestore
- Once you delete your cloud data, it is permanently removed from our servers
- We do not have the ability to recover deleted data

## Verification

After deleting your data:
- If you try to access your account from another device, you will need to sign in again
- Your data will no longer be synchronized across devices
- Other group members will no longer see your shared data

## Your Rights

Under data protection regulations (including GDPR if applicable), you have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Rectify inaccurate data
- Delete your data (right to be forgotten)
- Object to data processing
- Data portability

FoodNet is committed to respecting these rights and making data deletion simple and accessible.
